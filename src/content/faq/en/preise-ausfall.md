Fortunately, this rarely happens, because we can fly up to wind force 5 and in drizzle. If bad weather is foreseeable, we postpone the show at no extra cost – only travel that has already taken place is charged. If the show is cancelled completely, we keep at least the 40% deposit, because design and permit are already done by then. Depending on the timing, a scale from our terms and conditions applies (40 / 60 / 80 / 95%).
