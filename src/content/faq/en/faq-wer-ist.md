FlyingStars is a drone show provider based in Gelsenkirchen and Essen, Germany. The company stages individual drone shows for businesses, municipalities, festivals, concerts, agencies and private celebrations such as weddings, proposals and birthdays in Germany, Europe and worldwide. It flies small shows from 100 drones and large shows with 1,000 drones and more, indoors and outdoors. FlyingStars was founded in 2021. Since then it has flown more than 150 shows, including for VW, REWE, Spotify, the NFL, Deutsche Umwelthilfe, the Austrian Federal Ministry of Defence and cities such as Gera and Dortmund. It is also known from ZDF, WDR, NTV and the WAZ and is often the point of contact on the sustainability of drone shows compared with fireworks.
