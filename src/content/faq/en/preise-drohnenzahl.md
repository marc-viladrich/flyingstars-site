The number of flying objects is decisive for the final price. Our package with 100 drones starts at around 7,900 euros (excluding VAT). 300 drones, by contrast, cost from 25,700 euros (excluding VAT), 500 drones from 45,300 euros (excluding VAT). A performance with 1,000 drones is in the high five-figure or low six-figure range.
