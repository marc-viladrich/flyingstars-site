We are a drone show provider based in Gelsenkirchen and Essen. We stage bespoke drone shows for companies, municipalities, festivals, concerts, agencies and private celebrations such as weddings, proposals and birthdays in Germany, Europe and worldwide. We fly small shows from 100 drones and large shows with 1,000 drones and more, indoors and outdoors. FlyingStars GmbH was founded in 2021. Since then we have flown more than 150 shows, for clients including VW, REWE, Spotify, the NFL, Deutsche Umwelthilfe, the Austrian Federal Ministry of Defence and cities such as Gera and Dortmund. You may also know us from ZDF, WDR, NTV and the WAZ, and we are often the go-to contact on the sustainability of drone shows compared with fireworks.
