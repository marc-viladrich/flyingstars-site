Drone choreographies are usually a little more expensive than classic fireworks. While fireworks for events start at around 5,000 euros (excluding VAT), our most affordable package is 7,900 euros (excluding VAT). In return, the effect is stronger: most people are seeing the dancing light flyers for the first time – an experience you won't forget in a hurry.
