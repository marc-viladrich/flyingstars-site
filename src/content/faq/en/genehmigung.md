Yes. We hold an operating authorisation from Germany's Federal Aviation Office, valid throughout the EU. This generic authorisation lets us approve most flight sites ourselves. We handle additional applications, for example to German air traffic control.

We need consent from the landowners or, for public land, the city or municipality. We have a letter for this that we give you or send directly to the city.
