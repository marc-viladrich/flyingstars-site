Every drone in our swarm is equipped with a high-precision GPS module and LED lights. Complex software plans the flight path of each individual drone to the centimetre. A central computer on the ground sends the commands to the swarm in real time to ensure perfect synchronisation and smooth movements in the sky. The flight paths are pre-programmed; there is no real-time control. [More on how it works](/en/blog/2025-01-14-wie-funktioniert-drohnenshow-drohne/).
