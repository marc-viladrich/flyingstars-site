We start with a draft animation from take-off to landing. Then we edit the music to match it, either music free of GEMA royalties or your chosen tracks. Music editing is included in the price. Finally, we programme colours and light effects to the musical accents.

You or your event production team provide the on-site sound system. We send the music file in advance and give a countdown on the evening, then someone presses Play.
