At a show, hundreds of drones draw a logo, a line of lettering or a whole story across the sky. For each of them to light up in the right place at the right moment, we programme every drone individually beforehand.

We are FlyingStars, and we have been flying drone shows since 2021, more than 150 so far. Here we show you what we work with and how your show takes shape with us. For more on how a single drone works within the swarm, read [How does a drone show work?](/en/blog/2025-01-14-wie-funktioniert-drohnenshow-drohne/)

## What does "programming" mean for us?

Every drone gets its own flight path from us. Our show software fixes it in advance to the centimetre, along with its timing and light sequence. Nobody flies the drones by remote control during the show, and there is no real-time steering. Each drone flies exactly what we planned beforehand.

We do this planning ourselves. Alejandro from our design team turns your story into a choreography in 2D and 3D, and Patrick puts the fleet together and looks after the technical side. We don't bring in outside contractors for any of it.

## What we work with

Out on the show site, two things matter: our drones and the ground station.

### Our show drone

Each of our drones weighs only around 300 grams. The LED sits on top and becomes a point of light in the sky. GPS and a gyroscope keep the drone on its path and in balance. They carry no cameras, and their power comes from two ordinary AA rechargeable batteries, which we can swap individually, just like the motors and rotors.

### The ground station

Before take-off, all the drones stand in a grid on the ground. From the ground station we load the show onto every single one, give the start signal and keep an eye on the whole swarm. Each drone flies its path on its own. A digital fence, the geofence, keeps them inside the show area. If a drone has a problem, it usually flies back automatically. In an emergency it lands in the safety zone, where nobody is standing.

## How your show comes together

Your show comes together in five steps, and you can be as involved as you like.

### Idea and motif

We start by talking with you about the occasion and what you want to see in the sky. Together we develop the storyline, motifs and music. Our design team then builds the motifs as 3D models, for example a star, your logo or a piece of lettering.

### From motif to flight path

Each drone is one point of light. A single letter needs around 10 to 13 of them, a detailed logo more like 100 to 200. We assign the points of a motif to the drones and set each one's route from the take-off grid to its place, and on from there to the next image. The result is a rough animation from take-off to landing, still without any colour.

### Music and light

We cut the music along this rough animation, using royalty-free tracks or the music you choose. The music edit is included in the package. Finally, we place colours and light effects on the accents in the music, so flight path, timing and light all run on the same timeline.

### Simulation and test flight

Before the show goes onto the drones, we play it through completely in our software. We check the distances between the drones, and if two paths come too close, we change them. After that we do a test flight with real drones. Outdoors, the formation also has to cope with wind. Our drones hold their shape up to Beaufort force 5 and in light drizzle.

### The show evening

On the day, we start setting up around three and a half hours before the show. We keep the safety area of roughly 100 × 100 metres clear of anyone not involved while the show runs, and shortly before it we check GPS reception, weather and no-fly zones. Then we give your DJ or sound crew the countdown by radio or phone. At zero, someone presses "Play" and the drones lift off.

## How much time to plan for

Get in touch as early as you can. Two to three months of preparation are ideal, especially if the venue needs complex permits. If it is urgent, we can plan your show within one or two weeks for an express surcharge.

You or your technical crew provide the sound system on site. We send the finished, edited music in advance, for example as a WAV file, so on the evening only the countdown is left. If you book in the previous year, you get a 10 % early-booking discount.
