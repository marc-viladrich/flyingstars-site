Drone shows perform better than traditional fireworks in some aspects of environmental protection: they produce no harmful emissions on site, are quieter and are reusable. On the other hand, they affect the planet in other ways: they need electricity to fly (which is why we use green electricity), and raw materials are needed to produce them. Transport to the venue also plays a role. Unfortunately, we are not aware of any study that compares fireworks and drone dances across all environmental aspects. Our choreographies are therefore particularly suitable when noise and emission protection on site and reusability matter.
