All of them except the landowners’ consent. We hold a far-reaching permit from the German Federal Aviation Office (LBA) and an EU-wide operating licence. This allows us to carry out many shows – on private land, for example – without any additional approval procedure. We handle applications in more complex cases – for example with the German air traffic control (DFS) or for flights elsewhere in the EU – for you.
