Fireworks bang, smoke and leave debris on the ground. Dogs hide, small children cover their ears, and in dry weather they are frequently banned altogether. Our drone shows are often allowed to fly in exactly the places where fireworks are banned or unwelcome because of noise protection, fine dust or fire risk.

Event organisers often ask us how a drone show compares with fireworks. Here we answer with what we can back up, and we also name the points where a drone show leaves its own footprint.

## What a drone show is

In a drone show, anywhere from 100 to more than 1,000 illuminated drones fly one shared choreography. Each drone is a single point of light. Together they form images, lettering and figures in 2D and 3D that move and flow into one another.

The flight paths are fixed before the show starts, and we do not steer the drones in real time. Our design team first builds the animation from take-off to landing, still without colour, then we cut the music to fit it, and finally we set colours and light effects on its accents. Our shows usually last around 13 minutes, and up to 25 minutes is possible. As a rule, the drones stay below 120 metres.

What goes into a single drone is explained in our article on [how a drone show works](/en/blog/2025-01-14-wie-funktioniert-drohnenshow-drohne/). How an idea becomes a choreography is covered in [how we programme a drone show](/en/blog/2025-06-12-wie-programmiert-man-eine-drohnenshow/).

## Noise, smoke and litter compared

The difference is clearest on the night itself, where your audience is standing. Our drones only hum quietly, and your music sets the volume. TÜV NORD confirms our noise protection in an expert report. On site there is no fine dust, no nitrogen oxides and no heavy metals, and no open flame burns. The overview shows how drone shows, fireworks and laser shows compare.

After the show we pack the drones away, and they fly again at the next one. That keeps a drone in service for several years. When a part wears out, we replace just that part, such as a motor or a rotor. The same goes for the batteries. Each drone runs on two standard AA rechargeable batteries, the kind you know from household devices. When they lose capacity, we swap the batteries and keep the drone.

Each drone weighs only around 300 grams. We charge them with 100 % green electricity from naturstrom, the same supplier that powers our office. We drive to shows in an electric van.

A drone show does leave a footprint too. The drones need electricity, building them takes raw materials, and the journey to the venue counts as well. So far we know of no study that compares fireworks and drone shows across every environmental impact. That is why we mainly recommend our shows where noise protection, clean air on site and reusability matter to you. You can read more on our [sustainability](/en/nachhaltigkeit/) page.

## Logos and lettering in the sky

Fireworks cannot show a logo. With drones we write your brand name in the sky, show your product in 3D or tell a story across several images. One letter takes about 10 to 13 drones. For logos and lettering, 100 to 200 are usually enough, and for detailed 3D images we plan 300 or more.

For the launch of the new Ford Explorer, 350 of our drones wrote the name above the Electric Vehicle Center in Cologne. PUMA's request reached us 14 days before the derby, and 100 drones had the logo in the sky above Borussia-Park in time for kick-off. You approve every motif in advance, and we are happy to sign an NDA for agencies. Our page on [drone shows for advertising](/en/drohnenshow-werbung/) shows how campaigns with drones work.

Brands are only part of our work. We fly just as often for towns and municipalities, festivals and concerts, and private celebrations such as [weddings](/en/drohnenshow-hochzeit/), proposals or birthdays.

## Safety at our shows

Since we were founded in 2021, we have flown more than 150 shows without an accident. For every show we close off a safety area of around 100 × 100 metres, which the drones fly over and where they take off and land. Nobody from the audience is inside it during the show. A geofence, a kind of digital fence, keeps the drones within this area.

If a drone fails, which is rare, it usually flies back automatically. In an emergency it lands inside the safety zone, and it weighs only around 300 grams. Automatic and manual safety systems with several layers of backup monitor every flight. Our certified pilots and technicians are on site at every show. Our show drones carry no cameras.

Every package includes liability insurance of up to 1.3 million euros. We take care of the permits. We hold a generic authorisation from the German Federal Aviation Office (LBA) and an EU-wide operating licence, and we handle any further applications, for example with German air traffic control (DFS). All we need from you is consent from the owners of the land.
