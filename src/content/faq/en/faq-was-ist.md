A drone show is a choreography of illuminated drones. Pre-programmed drones automatically fly 2D and 3D motifs. Each drone forms one pixel that contributes to the overall image. A drone show (also called a drone dance or drone ballet) is therefore a popular alternative to fireworks or to laser and light shows.
